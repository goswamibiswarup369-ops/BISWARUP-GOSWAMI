In immediate danger, call 112 or 100 directly — a phone call is faster and more reliable than any app. Confirm the numbers active in your state, as some helplines vary by region.

Rakshak Line is a working prototype. It uses your device's own location and share tools —
it is not connected to any police dispatch system. Please treat it as a first step, not a replacement
for calling emergency services when you're in danger.
